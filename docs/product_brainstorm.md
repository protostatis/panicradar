## Differentiation Strategy

### Why Us vs. Fear & Greed Index?
1. **Multi-dimensional:** Not just one number, but activity/fear/euphoria breakdown
2. **Source quality:** Bayesian-weighted by accuracy, not equal weighting
3. **Volatility focus:** We predict vol (proven), they imply direction (unproven)
4. **Transparency:** We show which sources are reliable

### Why Us vs. Santiment/LunarCrush?
1. **Focused insight:** We don't overwhelm with 50 metrics
2. **Validated claims:** Everything we say is backtested
3. **Honest positioning:** We're a volatility indicator, not a crystal ball
4. **Affordable:** They charge $50-500/month, we can undercut

## Recommended First Product

**"Crypto Volatility Radar"** - Telegram Bot + Simple Dashboard

### Why This First?
1. **Leverages strongest finding:** Volatility prediction (r=0.30)
2. **Low development effort:** Bot + simple web page
3. **Clear value prop:** "Know when to expect big moves"
4. **Viral potential:** People share alerts in groups
5. **Easy monetization:** Free tier → paid alerts

### MVP Features
- Telegram bot: `/subscribe` to get alerts
- Alert when: Extreme sentiment detected → expect high vol
- Web dashboard: Current reading + 5-day vol forecast
- Freemium: 1 alert/day free, unlimited $5/month

### Launch Timeline

- Week 4: Iterate based on feedback
